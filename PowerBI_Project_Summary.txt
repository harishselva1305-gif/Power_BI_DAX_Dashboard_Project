POWER BI: DAX AND DASHBOARDS - PROJECT SUMMARY
==============================================

File name : Power_BI_DAX_Dashboard_Project.xlsx
Project   : Sales performance analytics (data model, DAX measures,
            interactive dashboard, publishing plan)
Dataset   : 300 sample orders (2024-2025), 4 regions, 3 categories,
            9 products, 60 customers (random sample data)

Note: Power BI Desktop illatha environment-la .pbix create panna mudiyadhu.
Adhanala ellam Excel-la build pannirukkom: dataset, ready-to-paste DAX code,
live Excel formulas (DAX result-a verify panna), dashboard demo.


SHEET BY SHEET
--------------

1. Overview
   - Topic-to-sheet map, Power BI Desktop-la rebuild panra 8 steps,
     data model (star schema), assumptions.

2. Sales_Data  (Fact table - Excel table name: Sales)
   - OrderID, OrderDate, Region, Category, Product, Segment, Customer,
     Quantity, UnitPrice, UnitCost, Discount
   - Calculated columns: Sales, Profit, Year, MonthNo, Quarter, MarginPct

3. Date_Table  (Calendar dimension - Excel table name: Calendar)
   - 731 days (2024-2025): Year, MonthNo, MonthName, Quarter, YearMonth,
     Weekday, IsWeekend. Power BI-la "Mark as date table" panna vendiyadhu.

4. DAX_Measures  (SUM, CALCULATE, FILTER, COUNTROWS)
   - 15 measures: Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Quantity, Order Count
     (COUNTROWS), Customer Count (DISTINCTCOUNT), AOV (DIVIDE),
     Profit Margin %, Technology Sales and West Sales 2025 (CALCULATE),
     High Value Orders (COUNTROWS + FILTER), Discounted Sales
     (CALCULATE + FILTER), Technology % of Total (ALL), Margin % (VAR/RETURN),
     Margin Status (IF), AOV Tier (SWITCH).
   - Ovvondrukkum: DAX code, Excel equivalent, live result, concept.
   - DAX function cheat sheet (12 functions).

5. Measure_vs_Column  (Measures versus calculated columns)
   - Comparison table: when calculated, memory, slicer response, use cases.
   - Live demo: average of row-level margin (29.49%) vs correct measure
     SUM(Profit)/SUM(Sales) (29.85%). Ratios measure-ah thaan irukkanum.

6. Time_Intelligence
   - Monthly table: MoM %, Sales PY, YoY %, YTD, Rolling 3M average.
   - DAX: SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR, DATEADD, TOTALYTD, TOTALQTD, TOTALMTD,
     DATESINPERIOD.
   - 2024 vs 2025 comparison, 2 charts.

7. Dashboard  (KPI cards, slicers, interactive reports)
   - 3 slicers (dropdowns): Year, Region, Category.
   - 6 KPI cards: Total Sales, Total Profit, Profit Margin (on/below
     target), Orders, Units Sold, Average Order Value.
   - 3 charts: Sales by Month, Region, Category.
   - Auto insights: top region, top category, best month, margin status.
   - Slicer maathina ellam recalculate aagum (cross-filtering).

8. Drill_Through  (Drill-through and tooltips)
   - Drill-through page: products for selected Region and Category.
   - Tooltip card: select a product to see sales, profit, margin,
     units, best region, share of total.
   - Drill-down: Year > Quarter > Month for selected Region and Category.

9. Design_Principles  (Dashboard design principles)
   - 13-point checklist with how each is applied in this project.
   - One-page layout wireframe (title, slicers, KPIs, trend, ranking,
     breakdown, detail table).

10. Publish_Share  (Publish, refresh and share)
   - Publish steps (Desktop to Service).
   - Refresh modes: Import, DirectQuery, Live connection, Incremental,
     Manual. Gateway notes.
   - Sharing: workspace roles, share link, App, Row-level security,
     export, embed.
   - 10-point pre-publish checklist with progress %.
